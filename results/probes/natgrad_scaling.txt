  READING:
  - In KL-relevant sigma units the contrast is the sharp one:
      vanilla PG / Pi_L=I : slope -2.00  (step in sigma units explodes as sigma -> 0)
      NGD / Pi_L=F_out    : slope +0.00  (scale-free -- sigma cancels exactly)
  - In RAW action units neither is flat (-1.00 vs +1.00): on-policy (a-mu) ~ sigma
    contributes its own power of sigma. A probe that regresses raw
    ||d_mu|| and expects 0 for correct NGD will read a false negative.
  - precision gap at the floor: F_out/I = 1/sigma^2 = 51.0x at sigma=0.14
